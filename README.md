# SportStreak# SportStreak

A simple and beautiful personal sports tracker to help you stay consistent.

Track your daily activity in **Table Tennis**, **Jogging**, **Running**, and **Swimming**.  
Build streaks, stay motivated with daily quotes, and watch your progress grow.

---

## Features

- Track 4 sports: Table Tennis, Jogging, Running, Swimming
- Daily streak system
- Motivational quote that changes every day
- Simple activity history
- Clean and modern dark design
- Fully responsive (works great on phone and computer)
- Data is saved in your browser (no account needed)

## How to Use

1. Open the website
2. Read the daily motivation quote
3. Click on any sport
4. Press **“I did it today!”** after completing the activity
5. Watch your streak grow

Your progress is automatically saved in your browser.
